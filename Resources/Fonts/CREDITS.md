# Font Credits

`Jost-Regular.ttf` / `Jost-SemiBold.ttf` -- [Jost*](https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost)
by indestructible type*, licensed under the **SIL Open Font License** (see `OFL.txt`) -- free to
use, modify, and embed, including commercially.

Chosen to match the vintage-synth-hardware look of the rest of the GUI: research into actual Moog
panel typography (Minimoog control panel = Futura all-caps; other Moog modules = News Gothic Extra
Condensed / Trade Gothic Condensed; Moog wordmark = Akzidenz Grotesk Extended) pointed to the same
family of 1920s-30s German geometric sans-serifs. Jost is modelled directly on that lineage
(Kabel/Futura) and is free/redistributable, unlike Futura itself.
